# FSRA Circular 2025/14 - Digital Lending and KYC Update Guidelines

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT - The "Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA)" is a fictional regulator created for the FinSight AI capstone. This is not a real regulatory circular. Its provisions are modelled on the structure of the RBI Digital Lending Directions and RBI KYC Master Direction for teaching purposes; some values match the real rules. Always refer to the official RBI / SEBI publications for actual regulatory requirements.

## 1. Applicability

These guidelines apply to all commercial banks, NBFCs and their lending service providers (LSPs) engaged in digital lending. The guidelines come into force from 1 September 2025.

## 2. Key Fact Statement (KFS)

Every regulated entity shall provide a Key Fact Statement to the borrower before execution of the loan contract. The KFS must disclose the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), the total cost of credit including all fees and charges, the recovery mechanism, and the details of the grievance redressal officer. Any fee or charge not mentioned in the KFS cannot be charged to the borrower.

## 3. Disbursal and Repayment

All loan disbursals shall be made directly into the bank account of the borrower. Disbursal through a pool account or third-party account of a lending service provider is prohibited. All repayments shall be made directly from the borrower's bank account to the regulated entity.

## 4. Cooling-off Period

Borrowers shall be given a cooling-off (look-up) period of at least 3 days for loans with a tenor of 7 days or more, and at least 1 day for loans with a tenor of less than 7 days. During this period the borrower may exit the loan by paying only the principal and the proportionate APR, without any penalty.

## 5. Grievance Redressal

Every regulated entity shall appoint a nodal grievance redressal officer for digital lending complaints. Complaints must be resolved within 30 days of receipt. If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the borrower may escalate it to the Regulatory Ombudsman.

## 6. Data Protection

Data collected through digital lending apps must be need-based and collected only with the prior explicit consent of the borrower. All data shall be stored only on servers located in India. Lending apps shall not access the borrower's contacts, photos or media files.

## 7. Periodic KYC Update (Re-KYC)

Regulated entities shall carry out periodic updation of KYC based on the customer's risk category: at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers, once every 8 years for medium-risk customers, and once every 10 years for low-risk customers. Customers shall be given at least three advance intimations, including at least one by letter, before the due date.

## 8. Penalties

Non-compliance with these guidelines may attract monetary penalties of up to ₹1 crore per instance, and the Authority may restrict the regulated entity from onboarding new digital lending customers.
