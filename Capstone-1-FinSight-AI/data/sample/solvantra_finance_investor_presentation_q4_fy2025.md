## Company Snapshot

Solvantra Finance is a diversified non-banking financial company (NBFC) focused on gold loans, MSME loans and two-wheeler loans in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns. The company operates 1,120 branches across 14 states and serves 42 lakh active customers.

## FY2024-25 Financial Performance

| Metric | FY2024-25 | FY2023-24 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assets Under Management (AUM) | ₹32,600 crore | ₹26,290 crore |
| AUM Growth | 24% | 21% |
| Net Profit | ₹1,045 crore | ₹842 crore |
| Return on Assets (ROA) | 2.9% | 2.8% |
| Return on Equity (ROE) | 16.4% | 15.9% |
| Cost of Funds | 8.6% | 8.9% |
| Capital Adequacy Ratio | 21.3% | 22.0% |

## AUM Mix

Gold loans form 38% of AUM, MSME loans 34%, two-wheeler loans 18% and other loans 10%. The gold loan book grew 31% during the year, helped by higher gold prices.

## Asset Quality

Gross Stage 3 (GS3) assets were 3.4% of AUM as of 31 March 2025, compared with 3.9% a year earlier. Net Stage 3 assets were 1.6%. The company maintains a provision coverage of 54% on Stage 3 assets.

## Borrowing Profile

The company's borrowings are diversified: bank term loans 52%, non-convertible debentures 28%, external commercial borrowings 9% and others 11%. The company's long-term credit rating was upgraded to AA (Stable) during the year.

## Guidance for FY2025-26

Management guides for AUM growth of 22-25%, ROA of 2.8-3.0% and stable cost of funds. The company plans to add 150 branches, mainly in South and West India.
