## Chairman's Message

FY2024-25 was a year of disciplined growth for Trivenza Bank. Net profit rose 18.4% year-on-year to ₹6,120 crore, the highest in the bank's history. Asset quality continued to improve, with Gross NPA declining to 2.1% from 2.6% a year earlier. We crossed the milestone of ₹5,62,000 crore in total deposits while keeping our CASA ratio above 40%.

The Board has recommended a final dividend of ₹12 per equity share of face value ₹2, compared with ₹10 per share for FY2023-24.

### Asset Quality

Gross NPA (GNPA) improved to 2.1% as of 31 March 2025 from 2.6% as of 31 March 2024. Net NPA (NNPA) improved to 0.6% from 0.8%. The Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) stood at 72%. Fresh slippages during the year were ₹4,950 crore, while recoveries and upgrades amounted to ₹3,880 crore.

### Capital Adequacy

The Capital to Risk-weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) was 17.8% as of 31 March 2025, of which Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) was 15.1%, well above the regulatory minimum.

## Segment Performance

Retail banking remains the bank's largest segment, contributing 58% of gross advances. The retail book grew 21% during the year, led by home loans (+19%) and personal loans (+26%). Corporate advances grew 9%, with the bank deliberately reducing exposure to stressed sectors. MSME advances grew 17% and now constitute 14% of the loan book.

The bank operates 1,840 branches and 4,600 ATMs across 28 states. During FY2024-25 the bank opened 155 new branches, 60% of which are in semi-urban and rural locations.

## Digital Banking

Mobile banking active users reached 2.1 crore, up from 1.6 crore in FY2023-24. Digital channels now account for 94% of all customer transactions. UPI transaction volume processed by the bank grew 48% year-on-year. The bank's AI-powered credit underwriting engine processed 62% of retail loan applications with instant decisions.

## Risk Management

### Credit Risk

The bank maintains a board-approved credit risk framework. Unsecured retail loans (personal loans and credit cards) constitute 11% of total advances. The bank tightened underwriting for unsecured loans in H2 FY2024-25 following an uptick in early delinquencies in the credit card portfolio.

### Liquidity Risk

The average Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) for Q4 FY2024-25 was 128%, against the regulatory requirement of 100%. The Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) was 119%.

### Key Risks for FY2025-26

Management has identified the following key risks: (1) margin compression if policy rates are cut, since 55% of advances are linked to external benchmarks; (2) rising stress in unsecured retail lending; (3) competition for deposits leading to higher cost of funds; and (4) cyber security and fraud risk in digital channels.

## Outlook

For FY2025-26, the bank targets advances growth of 16-18% and deposit growth of 15-17%, while aiming to keep the NIM in the range of 3.7%-3.9%. The bank plans to open 200 new branches and expects credit cost to remain below 0.8% of average advances.

## ESG and Sustainability

The bank's green financing portfolio reached ₹9,400 crore, financing renewable energy, electric vehicles and green buildings. The bank has committed to becoming carbon neutral in its own operations by FY2030. Women constitute 27% of the workforce.
