## Performance Summary for the Quarter ended 30 June 2025

Trivenza Bank reported a net profit of ₹1,710 crore for Q1 FY2025-26, up 15.2% year-on-year from ₹1,484 crore in Q1 FY2024-25. Net Interest Income (NII) grew 11.6% year-on-year to ₹4,020 crore.

The Net Interest Margin (NIM) for the quarter was 3.8%, compared with 3.9% in Q4 FY2024-25. The 10 bps compression was mainly due to the repo rate cut, which lowered yields on external-benchmark-linked loans faster than the bank's cost of deposits declined.

## Business Growth

Gross advances grew 17.1% year-on-year to ₹4,98,600 crore. Total deposits grew 15.8% year-on-year to ₹5,79,400 crore. The CASA ratio stood at 40.6%.

## Asset Quality

Gross NPA improved to 2.0% as of 30 June 2025 from 2.1% as of 31 March 2025. Net NPA stood at 0.6%. Provisions and contingencies for the quarter were ₹610 crore, compared with ₹540 crore in Q1 FY2024-25. Credit cost (annualised) was 0.5% of average advances.

## Capital

CRAR stood at 17.5% and CET1 at 14.9% as of 30 June 2025.

## Management Commentary

"Despite margin pressure from lower policy rates, we delivered healthy profit growth with stable asset quality. We remain on track to achieve our FY2025-26 guidance of 16-18% advances growth," said the Managing Director and CEO of Trivenza Bank.
